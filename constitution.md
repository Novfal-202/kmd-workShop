# Project Constitution: Corporate Policy & Expense Services
## Article I: The Spec-First Principle
1. No code shall be generated without an approved specification in `.specify/specs/`.
2. Code diffs that violate `spec.md` or introduce unrequested scope are rejected.
## Article II: Quality & Testing Standards
1. Every business policy rule MUST have a corresponding synthetic test case.
2. Code generated by agents MUST pass 100% of deterministic unit tests and static linters.
## Article III: Architectural Boundaries
1. Pure business logic functions MUST remain decoupled from HTTP/API transport layers.
2. Models MUST strictly use Pydantic v2 validation contracts.
